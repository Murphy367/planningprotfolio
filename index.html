    About

    I'm Murphy (Zhiyi) Tang. I studied Fashion Business Management at FIT, earning both my AAS and B.S. I read a garment from both sides: how it is built, and how it sells through.

    At FIT I worked across the merchandising cycle, from forecasting a Fall 2025 mood to writing purchase orders, planning six months of stock and sourcing gold from Peru. Since then I've applied the same thinking to data: sell-through forecasting at Moose Knuckles, and an M.S. in Integrated Design, Business and Technology at USC.
